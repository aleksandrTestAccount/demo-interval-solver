Karp's 1st NP-complete problem: Boolean Satisfiability (SAT). Demonstrates alternative inline notations for setting up standard conjunctive multi-variable boolean clauses that the engine must evaluate to TRUE. 

(x1 || !x2 || x3) && (!x1 || x3 || x4 || x5) && (x3) == true

// or 

x1 || !x2 || x3 == true
!x1 || x3 || x4 || x5 == true
x3 == true

// or 

x1 || !x2 || x3
!x1 || x3 || x4 || x5
x3
