Karp's 6th NP-complete problem: Set Covering. Finds the minimum number of subsets required to cover every element in the universal set u, by declaring constraints for the presence of each element across candidate subsets. 

//u = {1,2,3,4,5,6}

//a = {1,2,3}
//b = {2,3,4}
//c = {3,4,5}
//d = {4,5,6}
//e = {1,5,6}

a || e      //element 1 in [a, e]
a || b      //element 2 in [a,b]
a || b || c //element 3 in [a,b,c]
b || c || d //element 4 in [b,c,d]
c || d || e //element 5 in [c, d, e]
d || e      //element 6 in [d, e]

binarySearch min k 0 5

k = sumBool( a, b, c, d, e )
