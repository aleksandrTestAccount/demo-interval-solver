Karp's 11th NP-complete problem: 3-Satisfiability (3-SAT). Represents a configuration of boolean clauses limited to at most 3 literals each, modeled via native engine clause components. 

// (x1 || !x2 || x3) && (x1 || x2 || x4) &&  (!x1 || !x2 || x5) 

clause(x1, !x2, x3 )
clause(x1, x2, x4)
clause(!x1, !x2, x5)
